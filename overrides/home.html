FASTT

Feature-Adaptive Speech-to-Text Model Selection

Huseyin Karaca, A. Samil Namli, Suleyman S. Kozat

Bilkent University · Turk Telekom 6G R&D Laboratory

Pretrained speech recognizers fail on different clips. FASTT reads what their encoders compute, learns the feature space and the choice together under the word error rate it will pay, and runs only the chosen recognizer's decoder.
