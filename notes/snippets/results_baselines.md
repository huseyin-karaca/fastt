Every fusion ensemble falls below the best single model on AMI-SDM and Earnings-22 (negative GC). Only once is a method significantly better than <span class="nw">FASTT</span>: on LibriSpeech, where the three experts are all strong, weighted ROVER (0.0214 against 0.0224, p = 0.025). The QE-Router, which predicts each expert's error count and routes to the smallest estimate, is significantly behind <span class="nw">FASTT</span> on all four out-of-domain corpora (p ≤ 0.004). The raw XGBoost selector of AdaSTT, the same selector and objective without the learned transformation, is behind <span class="nw">FASTT</span> on all four, significantly on AMI-SDM (p = 0.015) and Earnings-22 (p < 0.001).
