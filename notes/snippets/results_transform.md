No transformation is best everywhere. For FASTT-Alternating the best is diagonal gating on VoxPopuli and AMI-SDM, and the linear projection on Earnings-22 and People's Speech, improving on the raw features by up to 0.34 WER points; for FASTT-Boosted it is the raw features on VoxPopuli, AMI-SDM and People's Speech, and the linear projection on Earnings-22, the boosted soft trees' splits being learned projections of the features themselves. The transformation is therefore a hyperparameter, with the identity among its values, which the main search chose per corpus and variant (underlined).
